Our Founding Story

On a Mission to Build Better Healthcare

“As patients and practitioners, we have all personally experienced the inefficiencies of our healthcare system.”

Founded in 2023, Rapport AI Medical has been on a mission to help make healthcare better for both providers and patients.

We work with healthcare and insurance providers to build customisable, AI-powered integration solutions that digitalise, streamline and enhance the medical care process at every level.

By leveraging AI and technology, we aim to empower healthcare providers to improve capacity, quality and efficiency—while giving patients better access, experiences and outcomes. We believe healthcare can be intuitive, accessible and efficient.
